INDEPENDENT PRACTITIONER'S REVIEW ENGAGEMENT REPORT ON SCHEDULE OF
REMUNERATION AND EXPENSES PAID TO CHIEF AND COUNCIL
To the Chief, Council and Membership
Fox Lake Cree Nation
We have reviewed the accompanying schedule of remuneration and expenses paid to Chief
and Council of Fox Lake Cree Nation for the year ended March 31, 2025. The schedule of
remuneration and expenses has been prepared by management of Fox Lake Cree Nation in
accordance with the requirements of the Indigenous Services Canada and Health Canada
Reporting Guide covering fiscal year 2024-2025.
Management's Responsibility for the Schedule of Remuneration and Expenses Paid to Chief
and Council
Management is responsible for the preparation and fair presentation of the schedule of
remuneration and expenses paid to Chief and Council in accordance with the requirements of
the Indigenous Services Canada and Health Canada Reporting Guide covering fiscal year
2024-2025, and for such internal control as management determines is necessary to enable
the preparation of the schedule of remuneration and expenses that is free from material
misstatement, whether due to fraud or error.
Practitioner's Responsibility
Our responsibility is to express a conclusion on the accompanying schedule of remuneration
and expenses paid to Chief and Council based on our review. We conducted our review in
accordance with Canadian generally accepted standards for review engagements, which
require us to comply with relevant ethical requirements.
A review in accordance with Canadian generally accepted standards for review engagements
is a limited assurance engagement. The practitioner performs procedures, primarily consisting
of making inquiries of management and others within the entity, as appropriate, and applying
analytical procedures, and evaluates the evidence obtained.
The procedures performed in a review are substantially less in extent than, and vary in nature
from, those performed in an audit conducted in accordance with Canadian generally accepted
auditing standards. Accordingly, we do not express an audit opinion on the schedule of
remuneration and expenses paid to Chief and Council.

Conclusion
Based on our review, nothing has come to our attention that causes us to believe that the
schedule of remuneration and expenses paid to Chief and Council does not present fairly, in
all material respects, the remuneration paid to Chief and Council in accordance with the
requirements of the Indigenous Services Canada and Health Canada Reporting Guide covering
fiscal year 2024-2025.
Basis of Accounting and Restriction on Distribution and Use
Without modifying our conclusion, the schedule of remuneration and expenses paid to Chief
and Council is prepared to satisfy the Indigenous Services Canada reporting requirements. As
a result, the schedule of remuneration and expenses paid to Chief and Council may not be
suitable for another purpose. Our report is intended solely for Fox Lake Cree Nation and
Indigenous Services Canada and should not be referred to or used for any other purpose.
