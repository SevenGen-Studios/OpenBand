Waywayseecappo First Nation
Notes to the Compiled Financial Statements
For the year ended March 31, 2025
1. Basis of accounting
The basis of accounting applied in the preparation of the reconciliation of Indigenous Services Canada (ISC) funded
programs of the Nation as at March 31, 2025 is on the historical basis, reflecting cash transactions with the addition of:
 accounts receivable less an allowance for doubtful accounts;
 accounts payable and accrued liabilities;
 prepaid expenses;
 deferred revenue;
 government assistance recognized as revenue when it is received;
 revenue is recognized when the goods are delivered to the customer, collection is probable, and the price is known;
 investment revenue is recorded when earned.
