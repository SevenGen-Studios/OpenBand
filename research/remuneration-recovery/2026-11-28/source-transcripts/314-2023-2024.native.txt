INDEPENDENT PRACTITIONER'S REVIEW ENGAGEMENT REPORT
To the Members and Chief and Council of Sapotaweyak Cree Nation:
We have reviewed the accompanying schedule of remuneration and expenses – Chief and Councillors of
Sapotaweyak Cree Nation for the year ended March 31, 2024.
Management's Responsibility for the Schedule
Management is responsible for the preparation and fair presentation of this schedule of remuneration and
expenses in accordance with the First Nations Financial Transparency Act, and for such internal control as
management determines is necessary to enable the preparation of a schedule of remuneration and
expenses that is free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error.
Practitioner's Responsibility for the Schedule
Our responsibility is to express a conclusion on the accompanying schedule of remuneration and expenses
based on our review. We conducted our review in accordance with Canadian generally accepted standards
for review engagements, which require us to comply with relevant ethical requirements.
A review of a schedule of remuneration and expenses in accordance with Canadian generally accepted
standards for review engagements is a limited assurance engagement. The practitioner performs
procedures, primarily consisting of making inquiries of management and others within the entity, as
appropriate, and applying analytical procedures, and evaluates the evidence obtained.
The procedures performed in a review are substantially less in extent than, and vary in nature from, those
performed in an audit conducted in accordance with Canadian generally accepted auditing standards.
Accordingly, we do not express an audit opinion on this schedule.
Conclusion
Based on our review, nothing has come to our attention that causes us to believe that the schedule of
remuneration and expenses – Chief and Councillors for the year ended March 31, 2024 is not, in all material
respects, in accordance with the First Nations Financial Transparency Act.
