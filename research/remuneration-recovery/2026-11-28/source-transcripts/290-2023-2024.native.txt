REVIEW ENGAGEMENT REPORT
To the Members of Sioux Valley Dakota Nation:
Management's Responsibility for the Schedule of Remuneration and Expenses
Practitioner's Responsibility
Conclusion
Chartered Professional Accountants
We have reviewed the accompanying Schedule of Remuneration and Expenses - Chief and Councilors of Sioux
Valley Dakota Nation for the year ended March 31, 2024. The Schedule has been prepared by management of the
Dakota Nation in accordance with the First Nations Financial Transparency Act.
Based on our review, nothing has come to our attention that causes us to believe that the accompanying schedule
is not prepared, in all material respects, in accordance with the reporting requirements of the First Nations
Financial Transparency Act.
Management is responsible for the preparation of the Schedule in accordance with the First Nations Financial
Transparency Act , and for such internal control as management determines is necessary to enable the preparation
of the Schedule that is free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error.
Our responsibility is to express a conclusion on the accompanying schedule based on our review. We conducted
our review in accordance with Canadian generally accepted standards for review engagements, which require us to
comply with relevant ethical standards.
A review of the Schedule in accordance with Canadian generally accepted standards for review engagements is a
limited assurance engagement. The practitioner performs procedures, primarily consisting of making enquiries of
management and other within the entity, as appropriate, and applying analytical procedures and evaluates the
evidence obtained.
The procedures performed in a review are substantially less in extent than, and vary in nature from those
performed in an audit conducted in accordance with Canadian generally accepted auditing standards. Accordingly,
we do not express an audit opinion on this schedule.
Saskatoon, SK
November 4, 2024

2. Basis of Presentation
a. Position
The nature of the position held by the elected person, i.e. Chief or councillor
b. Number of Months
The number of months during the fiscal year that the individual was a Chief or Councillor
c. Remuneration
d. Other Remuneration
e. Expenses
"Expenses" includes the costs of transportation, accommodation, meals, hospitality and incidental
expenses incurred by the individuals in fulfilling their duties
1. General
The Schedule has been prepared in accordance with the First Nations Financial Transparency Act . The
Schedule sets out the remuneration paid and the expenses reimbursed to the Chief and each Councillor
acting in their capacity, including their personal capacity - by the First Nation and by any entity that, in
accordance with generally accepted accounting principles, is required to be consolidated with the First
Nation. The schedule has been prepared in accordance with the First Nations Financial Transparency Act .
This Schedule includes transactions that occurred in activities carried out by Sioux Valley Dakota Nation as
described in Note #1 of the consolidated financial statements.
The information in this schedule is presented in accordance to the following definitions defined in the
First Nations Financial Transparency Act.
"Remuneration" means salaries other than the reimbursement of expenses earned in the capacity
as a Chief or Councillor.
"Other remuneration" includes earnings, wages, commissions, bonuses, fees, honoraria and
dividends and any other monetary benefits from employment by the First Nation or a consolidated
entity of the First Nation.
