Auditors' Attestation
To the Members of
Fort McKay First Nation
We have audited the statement of financial position of the Fort McKay First Nation as at March 31,
2024 and the statements of operations and accumulated surplus, changes in net financial assets and
cash flows for the year then ended, as reported on July 31, 2024.
Schedules of Salaries, Honoraria, Travel Expenses and Other Remuneration are required to be
presented to the membership of the Fort McKay First Nation pursuant to the funding agreement
between Indigenous Services Canada and Fort McKay First Nation. These schedules have been
compiled by the management of Fort McKay First Nation from their records. For the purposes of
understanding our involvement with these schedules, please note that:
• we have audited and separately reported an unqualified opinion on the financial statements;
• our audit was conducted for the purpose of forming an opinion on the financial statements taken
as a whole;
• the attached schedules are presented for the purposes of forming an opinion for the Membership
and the Department of Indigenous Services Canada and does not form part of the financial
statements; and
• the schedules have been subjected to the auditing procedures applied to the audit of the
financial statement taken as a whole.
During the course of the aforementioned audit, we encountered no discrepancies on these schedules.
However, no procedures have been carried out on these schedules in addition to those necessary to
form an opinion on the financial statements.
This report has been prepared in accordance with the applicable Assurance and Related Services
Guideline issued by the Chartered Professional Accountants of Alberta.
