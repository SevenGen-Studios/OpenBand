# Saskatchewan and Alberta 2024–25 remuneration OCR review

Reviewed November 16, 2026. This is a progress record for the next fiscal-year batch after Manitoba.

## Coverage

- Checked 69 Saskatchewan and 48 Alberta records. ISC listed 41 Saskatchewan and 21 Alberta remuneration filings. Twenty-eight Saskatchewan and 26 Alberta listings had no posted 2024–25 schedule. Whitefish Lake 128 uses a shared ISC identity and remains outside the individual schedule attribution.
- Downloaded all 62 listed sources. Ran RapidOCR on the candidate table and scan pages. The Beardy’s and Okemasis’ link opened consolidated statements and no individual remuneration table was located.
- Resolved and corrected ten schedules in this batch. A first OCR comparison is evidence for the review. The remaining schedules still require a complete sign-off on row coverage, column meaning, and applicable subtotals.
