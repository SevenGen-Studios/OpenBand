# Published 2025–26 remuneration schedules without OCR evidence

After the five manual-review schedules deployed, the next batch checked the 15 already-published 2025–26 schedules that lacked independent OCR. All 15 original ISC PDFs were retrieved and rendered. Fourteen schedules with 96 named rows passed independent verification. The tested rows survived manual override replay and sanitization.

| Nation | Correction |
|---|---|
| Misipawistik Cree Nation | Restored the expenses shown for all four officials. The sum across the schedule rose from $333,153 to $397,340. |
| Paul First Nation | Restored the separately reported business-enterprise amounts for all six officials. The middle column combines travel, allowances, reimbursements and other remuneration. The sum across the schedule rose from $1,691,726 to $1,702,574. |
| Wood Mountain First Nation | Separated travel from other remuneration in every row. The combined totals reconcile to the source. |
| Beardy’s and Okemasis’ Cree Nation | Separated per diem amounts from travel. The combined totals reconcile to the source. |
| Alexander First Nation | Recorded allowances and other payments as separately reported components. The source labels neither as travel. |

The latest queue still has three manual-review cases. Enoch’s managed entry derives several totals that differ by one dollar from the printed totals. Piapot’s source shows a separate unreimbursed member-payment adjustment. Fort McKay’s schedule gives amounts by role without naming the officials.
