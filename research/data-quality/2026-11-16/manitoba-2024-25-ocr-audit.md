# Manitoba 2024–25 chief and council remuneration OCR audit

Reviewed November 16, 2026. Scope is every 2024–25 remuneration listing returned by Indigenous Services Canada (ISC) for Manitoba’s 63 Nations.

## Coverage and method

- The 63 ISC listings contained 30 entries marked posted. Twenty-eight links returned a named remuneration schedule. Two links returned other financial documents. Thirty-three Nations had no posted 2024–25 schedule in their ISC listing.
- Read all 163 named chief and council rows in the 28 usable schedules. RapidOCR read rendered PDF pixels independently of the text layer. The rotated Long Plain scan was re-rendered upright for OCR. Sioux Valley’s scanned table was checked against its rendered image.
- Compared the individual names, roles, months, and payment columns. Reconciled printed totals, subtotals, and footer amounts where present. Inspected source images for OCR disagreements, including the six-month terms misread as nine months at Bloodvein.
- The current review branch adds seven newly recovered schedules and corrects eleven previously parsed schedules. The other ten usable schedules agree with the current review branch, which already contains the Cross Lake, Pinaymootang, and Fisher River repairs.
