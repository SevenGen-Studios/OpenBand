## Preservation and boundaries

All pre-existing SK, AB and MB records and source geometry are preserved by the transaction baseline. 1602 validated BC display parcels were added using the existing geometry validator. Parcel ownership and identifiers are retained; display geometry is not a legal survey.

No BC filings, PDFs, OCR, financial summaries, totals or remuneration were collected. Financial fields remain unindexed, not zero.

## Limitations

- The profile count spans overlapping band/village, national and community-administration levels; do not sum their populations.
- Historical map and newer treaty evidence can disagree. Non-signatory status is never inferred from absent data.
- Unresolved hereditary/governing-authority identities remain in the review queue; broad roster reconciliation is not claimed complete.
- Three logos have visual/source/hash approval; remaining candidates use initials pending review.
- Source-listed websites have provenance but are not a guarantee of current availability.
